Project Statement Overview 

The office is located in a new building and is currently under construction.

At this stage, we would like to receive your proposal for an optimal technical solution, as well as a preliminary quote for the project.

Please take the following requirements into account during the design phase:

The office will have:
22 engineering workstations;
2 workstations for managers;
1 workstation for the Office Manager;
2 guest workstations.
All workstations will be equipped to the same standard.
Typical equipment for a single workstation includes:
a laptop with a docking station;
two external monitors;
an electrically adjustable desk;
standard peripheral devices.
Please provide a sufficient number of electrical outlets and LAN (RJ45) ports for each workstation, as well as a reasonable reserve for future expansion.
The office will feature a raised floor. We prefer that all electrical and structured cabling be routed under the raised floor.
If possible, we prefer to make maximum use of existing electrical outlets to power the new installation.
Please propose an optimal solution regarding:
the number and location of floor boxes;
the number and location of electrical outlets;
the number and location of LAN outlets.
